Context

The Levels 0–5 ladder

Every situation maps to a Level. Every Level has a matching Formula.

0

Home / Off-Duty

Comfortable clothes, sneakers, and casual habits still have a place.

1

Everyday Public

Errands, casual outings, ordinary public life. A legitimate, comfortable answer.

2

Elevated Casual

Coffee meetings, casual Fridays, weekend social plans.

3

Office / Client-Facing

Client contact, presentations, more public-facing work.

4

Formal Occasion

Weddings, interviews, family photographs, important meetings.

5

High-Stakes / Representing Others

Moments where you dress for the importance of someone else's occasion, not your own.

Personalization

Neutral base → accent → echo

Safe Foundation + Controlled Accent, shown in three steps.

1

Neutral base

Start with a safe, versatile foundation that works with almost everything.

2

One controlled accent

Add a single deliberate color or texture that stands out on purpose.

3

One echo

Repeat a small note of that accent once more, tying the outfit together without overdoing it.
