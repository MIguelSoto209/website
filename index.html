Five ideas. Repeat until you know them.

Everything in the book supports one of five concepts. Learn these, and you have the whole operating system.

Levels + Formulas

Levels tell you how dressed up the moment is. Formulas tell you what to put on. Two decisions, not a hundred.

The 85% Principle

The Author

I was this guy too.

During the most isolated stretch of 2020, my world got small — almost no client contact, few social reasons to dress up, even haircuts became a do-it-yourself job. My wardrobe was fine for the life I was living. Then life started asking more of it again.

This isn't a story about dressing badly and fixing myself. My circumstances changed, my wardrobe stopped keeping up, and I built a system to regain range — one I now teach to men who don't want fashion to become a hobby.
