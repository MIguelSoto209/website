Creator Partnerships

Help, not criticism — content your audience will actually save.

We prioritize fit over follower count. If your audience is practical, busy, career-focused, midlife, family-oriented, budget-aware, or style-curious without being fashion obsessives, this is built for you.

Request the Creator Kit

Men's Audience

Content experiments that perform.

  • “Can six purchases fix my wardrobe?” — follow the Momentum Six for one week.
  • “What Level is this?” — test five real-life situations and choose the correct Level.
  • “I hate dress shoes” — compare a comfort-first mature shoe to your old default.
  • “Random outfit vs. Formula outfit” — same closet, different decision method.
  • “The 85% test” — identify the menswear knowledge you actually use vs. specialty knowledge you don't need yet.

Women's Audience

Framed as relief and partnership.

Give him a system he can own — instead of buying isolated pieces or becoming responsible for dressing him.

  • “Can six purchases give my husband one full week of easier outfits?”
  • “The gift for a man who says he doesn't need clothes.”
  • “The gift that helps him without trying to change him.”
  • “I stopped picking his outfits — I gave him a system.”
  • “Three things that make him look more intentional without making him dressy.”
  • “Before the date / after the Formula” visual comparison.

What's In the Kit

Everything you need, nothing you didn't ask for.

  • Digital advance copy or finished book
  • One-page “What This Book Is” sheet with the five hero concepts
  • 5–10 approved vertical images/reels and 5 square/carousel assets
  • Momentum Six challenge card
  • Levels 0–5 one-page graphic
  • AI Wardrobe Assistant demo access or short demo video
  • Three approved description options: practical, humorous-light, and gift-oriented
  • Tracked link / QR code / referral code, if used
  • A clear statement: honest feedback is welcome, a positive review is never required
