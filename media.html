Short bio

Miguel Soto is the author of From Sneakers to Sharp, a practical wardrobe operating system for regular men who want to know how dressed up to be, what to wear, and what to buy next — without becoming fashion guys. After nearly twenty years in insurance and business, Miguel rebuilt his own wardrobe from the ground up and turned what worked into a repeatable system: Levels + Formulas, the 85% Principle, the Momentum Six, and Safe Foundation + Controlled Accent. He speaks with a peer voice, not a style-authority voice — “I was this guy too.”
