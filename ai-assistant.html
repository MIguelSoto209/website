What Makes It Different

Other apps know what's in the closet.

From Sneakers to Sharp is built to be known for understanding what the moment requires — role, audience, stakes, and local context, not just an inventory of owned clothes.

How We'll Build It

A few commitments, up front.

  • An explicit “do not overbuy” rule — your closet comes first; purchases are recommended only when a real gap exists.
  • Fit is something you verify physically. The assistant can guide fit, but it cannot guarantee it.
  • Not tied to one AI vendor or platform — the master rules live in a controlled source document.
  • Privacy around wardrobe photos, fit information, and account data is planned before any broad release.
